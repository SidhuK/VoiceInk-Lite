# Shortcuts

Set your shortcuts in Settings, under Shortcuts. Click the shortcut box, then press what you want to use.

## What you can use

- A key with modifiers, such as ⌥Space or ⌃⌘D.
- A single modifier key on its own, such as Right ⌘, Right ⌥, or Fn. Left and right are told apart, so Right ⌘ won't fire when you use Left ⌘ for copy and paste.
- A Hyper key. If Karabiner, Hyperkey, or a similar app turns Caps Lock into ⌃⌥⇧⌘, press it like any other key. It shows up as "Hyper".
- F1 to F20 on their own.
- The key left of 1 on its own. That's ` on US keyboards and § on UK and European ones.
- Mouse buttons other than left and right click, such as the middle button or side buttons.

A plain letter or number on its own isn't allowed, because you'd lose the ability to type it. Shortcuts macOS already uses, like ⌘C or ⌘Q, are blocked too.

## Ways to hold the shortcut

Next to each recording shortcut is a menu with four choices.

| Choice | How it works |
| --- | --- |
| Toggle | Press once to start, press again to stop. |
| Push to Talk | Hold to record, let go to stop. |
| Hybrid | A quick tap works like Toggle. Hold it for half a second or more and it works like Push to Talk. |
| Double Tap | Tap twice quickly to start, tap twice again to stop. Good for keys you also press by accident. |

If you press the recording shortcut and then type another key within a second, the app treats it as an accident and cancels. This doesn't apply to Double Tap.

## Using the Fn key

The 🌐 Fn key works as a shortcut, but macOS also has its own job for it. By default, pressing it opens the emoji picker or switches your keyboard language. Both happen at the same time as your recording.

To stop that:

1. Open System Settings, then Keyboard.
2. Set "Press 🌐 key to" to "Do Nothing".

VoiceInk Lite shows a reminder with a button to open Keyboard settings when you use Fn and this isn't set yet.

Fn only works on Apple keyboards and MacBooks. Most other keyboards handle Fn inside the keyboard and never tell the Mac about it.

## Using ` or §

When the key left of 1 is your shortcut, pressing it on its own no longer types `. Shift still types ~ as usual. If you write a lot of code or Markdown, pick something else.

## Other shortcuts

- Additional Shortcuts in Settings can paste your last transcription, paste the last AI-enhanced version, or retry the last one.
- Each mode can have its own shortcut, which switches to that mode and starts recording. Set it under Triggers on that mode's page.
- While the recorder is on screen, ⌥1 to ⌥0 switch modes. See [The recorder](recorder.md#switching-modes-from-the-keyboard).
