# The recorder

The recorder is the small window that shows up while you dictate. There are two styles. Pick one in Settings, under Interface, then Recorder Style.

## Mini recorder

A small black pill near the bottom of the screen.

- ✕ on the left cancels the recording. When nothing is recording, it closes the recorder.
- The bars in the middle move with your voice. They pulse slowly while your words are being processed.
- ✓ on the right finishes the recording and pastes the text.

Hover over the pill and it opens up to show two things.

- Your modes, as a row of buttons. The one in use is highlighted. Click another to switch. This works mid-recording too.
- The live text, if you're using a model that transcribes as you speak.

Move the pointer away and it shrinks back. While you're talking and not hovering, you only see the pill, so the text doesn't distract you.

You can also right-click the pill for a menu of modes.

## Notch recorder

A strip that grows out of the notch at the top of the screen.

- The button on the left starts and stops recording.
- The mode button next to it shows the current mode. Hover over it for the list of modes.
- Hover over the strip to see the live text.

## Switching modes from the keyboard

While the recorder is on screen, ⌥1 through ⌥9 switch to your first nine modes, and ⌥0 to the tenth. The order matches the list in the Modes page.

## Live text settings

Settings, then Interface, has two switches.

- Live Text Display turns live text on or off.
- Only Show Live Text on Hover is on by default. Turn it off to show live text all the time while you record.

Live text only appears with models that transcribe as you speak. Models that transcribe at the end show nothing until you finish.

## Cancelling

Press Esc twice to cancel a recording. You can change this in Settings, under Additional Shortcuts, then Cancel Recording.
